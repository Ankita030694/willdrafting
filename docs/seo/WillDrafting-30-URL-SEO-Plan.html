1. What the Research Showed

1.1 Where the search demand actually is

The current 18 URLs cover "I want to make a Will" intent. Most succession search volume in India sits in 3 other clusters, and none of our pages target them yet.

Demand ClusterExample QueriesShare of Category VolumeCovered Today?

B. "Give me a format" (templates)will format, will format pdf, वसीयतनामा फॉर्मेट, release deed format, living will formatVery large, strong link magnetNo (only the doctor-certificate template inside Blog #10)
C. "What are my rights?" (family property rights)daughter's right in father's property, second wife children property rights, stridhan, HUF after karta's deathLarge, emotional, high share ratePartly (Blog #7 ancestral property)
D. "I want to make a Will" (core product intent)online will india, will registration, NRI willSmaller but highest conversionYes (18 URLs)
Strategic takeaway
Clusters A–C bring in the traffic. Cluster D turns it into revenue. The plan uses A–C as entry points and sends visitors to /start, /pricing and AMA Legal service pages through internal links. A person settling a parent's estate is the warmest possible lead for writing their own Will.

1.2 Competitor gaps

From auditing WillJini, iWills, AasaanWill, SaralWill, GetYellow, SmartWill, LawRato and NoBroker:

  1. Nobody has a clean Hindi will-format page with a legally accurate Section 63 explanation. The ranking pages are thin template dumps (rajsevak, nyaymitra, scribd).
  2. Legal heir certificate SERPs are dominated by state portals and generic property sites. None link the certificate to what to do next (mutation, demat, bank claim), so there's an opening for a full post-death roadmap.
  3. SEBI demat transmission thresholds and the RBI UDGAM claim process are covered by banks and brokers in jargon, with no plain-language family guide.
  4. The Urmila Dixit (SC, Jan 2025) Section 23 gift-deed-revocation ruling hasn't been turned into a parent-facing "gift deed vs Will" decision guide by any Will platform.
  5. Unmarried / childless / single testators (including partners with no statutory inheritance rights) are almost entirely ignored, even though a Will is their only way to choose heirs.
  6. Digital assets / crypto in a Will: only GetYellow covers it seriously. Early-mover opportunity.
  7. Tools: iWills uses 4 tools as entry points; we have 1 (the succession calculator). Checklists and checkers are cheap to build and convert well.
