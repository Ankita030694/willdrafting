DOCUMENT PURPOSE

This document consolidates the complete product idea and all major
decisions discussed for WillDrafting.com. It is intended to be shared
with the product team, UI/UX designer, developers, legal team, reviewing
lawyers, and business stakeholders.

The product is a legal-tech platform that helps ordinary people prepare
their own Will by answering simple questions. The platform converts
structured user responses into a professionally structured Will,
performs automated validation and legal health checks, and sends the
draft to a lawyer for review. After lawyer review and any required
clarification or correction, the user receives the final Will document
and execution guidance.

The product should not be positioned merely as a cheap online Will
generator. It should be positioned as:

“Your wishes. Your family. Legally protected.”

The emotional positioning may be used in selected marketing areas, but
the actual product should feel like a serious LegalTech/SaaS platform.

A strong emotional brand line discussed earlier was:

“It’s not about what you leave behind. It’s about who you leave it for.”

This line can be used selectively on the homepage or campaign pages,
while the product interface should maintain legal authority, clarity,
trust, privacy, and professional structure.

1. PRODUCT VISION

WillDrafting.com should become a legal document automation and
estate-planning platform with a website attached to it.

The core experience:

User answers simple questions -> Structured data is created -> Rules
engine validates the information -> Approved legal clauses are selected
-> A Will draft is assembled -> Automated legal health checks are
performed -> A lawyer reviews the draft -> Clarifications/corrections
are handled -> Final Will is generated -> User receives the final
document and execution instructions -> The Will is stored in a secure
Will Vault -> The user can later review or update the Will

The core product promise:

“Tell us your wishes in simple questions. We take care of the legal
drafting. A lawyer reviews your Will before it is finalised.”

The technology is the engine. The lawyer creates confidence. The
interface makes the process simple.

2. STRATEGIC POSITIONING

The product should combine:

-   Trust of a law firm
-   Simplicity of a fintech app
-   Intelligence of legal technology
-   Professionalism of a document-management platform
-   Human understanding of family and legacy

The product should not feel like:

-   A generic law-firm website
-   A government website
-   A complicated legal form
-   A cheap document generator
-   A generic SaaS dashboard
-   An AI chatbot that freely invents legal language
-   An emotionally manipulative campaign

The recommended balance is:

-   60% trust and simplicity
-   25% emotional connection
-   15% legal expertise

Emotion should open the door. Technology should make the process easy.
Lawyer review should create confidence.

25. LEGAL HEALTH CHECK

Other possible checks: - Beneficiary allocation not equal to 100% - No
executor selected - Executor is unavailable or potentially unsuitable -
No alternate executor - Minor beneficiary without relevant

Duplicate beneficiaries - Contradictory instructions - Asset assigned to

revocation not addressed - Special wishes that may not be legally
enforceable - Missing residuary clause - Missing alternate beneficiary -
Incomplete identity information - Unverified user declarations

The health check should not claim to guarantee legal validity. It is a
structured quality and completeness check.

26. AI AND AUTOMATION PRINCIPLES

Do not allow an unrestricted AI model to freely write the final Will.

Avoid: “User answers -> ChatGPT -> Write a Will”

Recommended approach:

Structured data -> Rules engine -> Approved clause library -> Document
assembly -> Lawyer review

AI may assist with: - Classifying user responses - Asking clarifying
questions - Detecting inconsistencies - Identifying missing
information - Explaining legal terminology in plain language -
Suggesting which module to open - Summarising user-provided
information - Flagging unusual or complex cases for lawyer review -
Assisting lawyers during review

AI should not independently: - Invent legal clauses - Decide legal
outcomes without approved rules - Determine enforceability
conclusively - Replace lawyer review - Change legal meaning silently -
Produce untracked edits - Make unsupported claims about registration,
inheritance, guardianship, or validity

All generated legal content should come from approved templates,
controlled rules, and lawyer-reviewed clause versions.

28. RULES ENGINE

The rules engine decides: - Which questions appear - Which questions are
required - Which clauses are selected - Which warnings are generated -
Which cases require lawyer escalation - Which document template
applies - Which fields must be confirmed

Example:

Question: Are you married? If no: - Do not ask spouse-specific
questions. If yes: - Ask spouse name and relevant details. - Ask whether
spouse is a beneficiary. - Ask whether spouse is executor.

Question: Do you have children? If no: - Skip child module. If yes: -
Ask number of children. - Ask each child’s details. - Determine
minor/adult status. - If minor, activate guardian module.

Question: Is any property jointly owned? If yes: - Ask ownership nature
and share. - Generate a warning. - Consider mandatory lawyer review.

Question: Are any beneficiaries minors? If yes: - Ask age and relevant
information. - Show minor beneficiary warning. - Ask for relevant
distribution/guardian instructions.

29. DOCUMENT GENERATION PIPELINE

normalisation -> Rules engine -> Legal health checks -> Clause selection
-> Template assembly -> DOCX generation -> PDF generation -> Document

Secure storage

Do not store only the final PDF. Store the structured data and all
versions.

This is necessary so that the user can later update the Will without
starting over.

47. SECURITY AND PRIVACY

Because the product handles highly sensitive personal, family,
financial, and legal information, security must be designed from the
beginning.

Consider: - Encryption in transit - Encryption at rest - Strong
authentication - Optional multi-factor authentication - Role-based
access control - Least-privilege access - Secure document storage -
Signed URLs or equivalent controlled access - Audit logs - Session
expiry - Account recovery controls - Data retention policy - Data
deletion policy - Privacy policy - Consent records - Lawyer access
logging - Admin access logging - Backup and disaster recovery - Secure
file scanning - Protection against document enumeration - Protection
against insecure direct object references - Rate limiting - Monitoring
and incident response

Do not claim absolute security. Use accurate and reviewable language.

56. PRODUCT PRINCIPLE

Every screen should answer one clear question.

Avoid one giant form containing: - Name - Date of birth - Address -
Marital status - Children - Parents - Employment - Assets -
Beneficiaries

Instead: “What’s your full name?” Continue. “When were you born?”
Continue. “Are you married?” Continue.

The experience may technically contain many steps, but it should feel
psychologically easy.

57. BRAND AND COPY PRINCIPLES

Use plain language.

Instead of: “Enter beneficiary details.”

Use: “Who should receive this?”

Instead of: “Appoint a testamentary executor.”

Use: “Who would you trust to carry out your wishes?”

Instead of: “Input residuary beneficiary.”

Use: “Who should receive anything not specifically mentioned?”

Instead of: “Generate testamentary instrument.”

Use: “Prepare your Will.”

Legal terminology may be shown with an explanation when necessary.

58. CORE DIFFERENTIATOR

The signature feature should be the Legal Health Check.

The product should not merely collect answers and produce a document.

It should: - Detect missing information - Detect inconsistent
instructions - Detect allocation problems - Detect minor beneficiaries -
Detect joint ownership - Detect missing residuary planning - Detect
executor gaps - Detect guardian gaps - Detect complex assets - Escalate
cases to lawyers - Give users a clear explanation of what needs
attention

This is what makes the product genuinely LegalTech.

60. FINAL RECOMMENDATION

Start with the legal logic, not the website.

The correct order is:

1.  Define legal scope
2.  Build questionnaire and decision tree
3.  Build clause library
4.  Build legal rules
5.  Build legal health checks
6.  Design user experience
7.  Build structured database
8.  Build document engine
9.  Build lawyer portal
10. Build customer dashboard
11. Test real scenarios
12. Launch a controlled MVP
13. Expand based on lawyer and customer feedback

The website and UI are important, but they are not the core intellectual
property.

The core intellectual property is: - Structured legal data model -
Questionnaire decision tree - Clause library - Rules engine - Legal
health-check system - Lawyer review workflow - Version-controlled
document engine - Secure Will Vault

The product should ultimately feel like:

A trusted law firm on the outside, a simple fintech-style workflow in
the middle, and a sophisticated legal rules and document engine
underneath.

The final guiding statement:

“WillDrafting.com is not just a website that generates a PDF. It is a
legal-tech platform that helps people express their wishes clearly,
checks their information systematically, brings in a lawyer when needed,
and keeps their Will organised for the future.”
